# Built-in IP combinations

The following combinations are built in and may be selected independently for
the LOGO primary visual and cover:

- `author-only`
- `tuotuo-only`
- `xingbi-only`
- `tuotuo-xingbi`
- `author-tuotuo`
- `author-xingbi`
- `author-tuotuo-xingbi`

The three IPs are peers. A combination choice for one output does not silently
change the choice for the other output.
