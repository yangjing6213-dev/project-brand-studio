# Built-in IP combinations

The following combinations are built in and may be selected for each confirmed
platform cover:

- `author-only`
- `tuotuo-only`
- `xingbi-only`
- `tuotuo-xingbi`
- `author-tuotuo`
- `author-xingbi`
- `author-tuotuo-xingbi`

The three IPs are peers. A combination choice for one platform cover does not
silently change the choice for another cover. There is no separate square LOGO
visual output in the new workflow.
